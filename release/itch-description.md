Simple real-time strategy, with roguelite elements, for the browser.

Explore, choose where to build your base, gather resources, raise an army, and destroy rivals. Each expedition victory earns a benefit for the battles ahead. Recover and invest Echo in permanent upgrades. Later stages become free-for-all battles against multiple AI opponents.

Victory is only part of the journey: build homes and research facilities to raise your Civilization Score, unlock deeper stages or enjoy decorating the landscape.

- Single-player expeditions with lasting benefits and permanent fleet upgrades
- Settlement building and Civilization Score progression across conquered worlds
- Several landscape families with varied terrain and a day/night cycle
- Mouse and touch controls

Development prototype! Progress and battle saves are stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser storage removes your saves. There are still bugs and performance issues.
