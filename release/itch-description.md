A touch-focused real-time strategy roguelite for the browser.

Explore the terrain, choose where to build your base, gather Cinder and Echo, raise an army, and destroy rival headquarters. Each expedition victory earns a benefit for the battles ahead. Recover Echo and invest it in permanent fleet upgrades for future expeditions. Later stages become free-for-all battles against multiple AI opponents.

Victory is only part of the journey: build homes and research facilities to raise your Civilization Score and unlock deeper stages. Return to conquered worlds to expand your settlements throughout the expedition. Defeat ends the run, but your permanent fleet upgrades remain.

Play as three factions with distinct doctrines: the fortified Cinder Pact, the numerous Manyroot, and the technology-focused Mourning Houses. The latter two unlock as you advance. Choose four Command abilities at the start of each expedition to shape your strategy.

Fight across six procedurally generated landscape families: Desert, Alien Planet, Mothership, Westmark, Frontier, and Haven. Terrain, resource placement, and layouts vary between battles.

- Single-player expeditions with lasting benefits and permanent fleet upgrades
- Settlement building and Civilization Score progression across conquered worlds
- Six landscape families with varied terrain and a day/night cycle
- Enemy AI that builds and pays for its forces, scouts, and uses its own vision
- Mouse and touch controls
- Autosaved battles and revisitable conquered worlds within each run

Development prototype: Single-player only. Progress and battle saves are stored locally in your browser, with no cloud synchronization. Reloading lets you resume the latest saved battle, paused; clearing browser storage removes your saves.
