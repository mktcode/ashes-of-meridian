# Beyond the battlefield

Expeditions are becoming more than a sequence of battles. You can now return to conquered worlds and keep building: homes, research facilities, and the new Meridian Forum turn your bases into growing settlements.

These civilian buildings contribute to your **Civilization Score** across the expedition. Reaching the next stage now takes both military victory and enough civilian development. You can build ahead on earlier worlds or return after a victory to meet the next target. Conquered worlds stay fully revealed, with no fog of war.

A few other improvements:

- The home screen shows your saved world, including its buildings and time of day.
- Completed buildings can be rotated to arrange your settlement.
- Softer building lights and wet-ground highlights give settlements more atmosphere at night.
- Touch box-selection is available through hold-and-drag, and the opening tutorial now explains supply limits and depot expansion.

Still a development prototype—feedback on the new balance between fighting and building is especially welcome!
